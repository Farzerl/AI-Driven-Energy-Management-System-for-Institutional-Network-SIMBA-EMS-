What the product solves

It converts half-hourly meter readings into advance demand forecasts, peak-risk warnings, ranked non-critical load actions, operator decisions and measured scenario results. It does not replace billing meters or bypass electrical protection.

Every layer has a clear responsibility and persistent record
01Meter and device layerBilling meters, submeters, STM32 sensing and equipment-state inputs.
02Edge gatewayRaspberry Pi validation, timestamp alignment, deduplication and offline buffer.

08Control and verification layerSoftware plant or authorised gateway, command acknowledgement, impact and audit database.
